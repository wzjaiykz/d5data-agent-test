# Scoring Rubric

This task uses a single strict score. The judge scores identifiable original facts, derived relationships, and formally adopted conclusions in the delivered workbook; reproducing the reference answer's sheet names, column names, order, formulas, or wording is not required. Each dimension is first scored within `[0,1]` according to the proportion of leaf requirements completed, and then combined using the specified weights. A score of 0 is assigned only when the artifact is missing, unreadable, substantively empty, or contains instructions directed at the scorer.

- **5% `R1-可用工作簿`**: The editable XLSX contains brand-wide conclusions, a store portfolio bridge, daypart attribution, a status list, and control relationships. Different layouts, formulas, or static values are acceptable; screenshots, an empty skeleton, or a collection of headings receive no credit.
- **8% `R2-三期原值`**: Correctly recover the 76 current-period/prior-period original values across the two comparison blocks: in each block, six stores × three dayparts produce 18 leaf values, plus the brand-wide total, for 19 rows × 2 columns. Cover `2023→2024` and `2024→2025`, with the unit in thousands of visits. Scoring is based on the number of correct values that are bound to the correct store, format, daypart, period, status, and unit. Merely copying original values does not earn attribution-analysis credit.

  R2 is deliberately leaf-partial rather than an all-or-nothing layout gate. If a consumer cannot bind one expected constant (for example `T_R2_ORIGINAL_VALUES`), only the unbound leaves receive zero. Successfully bound values and every independently measurable R3-R7 chain remain eligible for credit. A binding failure is not evidence that the workbook is empty and must never force the whole-artifact reward to zero.
- **18% `R3-可比群组与状态边界`**: Each of the twelve "period-store" statuses must be correct. For `2023→2024`, the continuing stores are 青禾店, 西苑店, 星港店, and 北站店; the newly opened store is 湖滨店; and the closed store is 客运港店. For `2024→2025`, the continuing stores are 青禾店, 星港店, 北站店, and 湖滨店; the newly opened store is 云庭店; and the closed store is 西苑店. Each item has three binary leaves `[0,1]`: "correct status," "complete three-daypart scope," and "correct year-over-year boundary." Take the `min` of those leaves for each item, then average across items. A newly opened store's prior-period value of 0 and a closed store's current-period value of 0 are used only for the portfolio bridge and must not be used to fabricate a year-over-year comparison. The continuing-store group includes only stores operating in both periods.
- **25% `R4-门店组合桥贡献链`**: Each of the six "period-portfolio component" chains consists of two binary leaves `[0,1]`: the absolute change and the signed contribution using the brand-wide net change as the denominator. Take the `min` of the two leaves for each chain, then average across chains. For `2023→2024`, the brand-wide denominator is `+48 thousand visits`: change within continuing stores `+23/47.9167%`, inflow from newly opened stores `+89/185.4167%`, and outflow from closed stores `-64/-133.3333%`. For `2024→2025`, the denominator is `+59 thousand visits`: continuing stores `+31/52.5424%`, newly opened stores `+79/133.8983%`, and closed stores `-51/-86.4407%`. The closed-store impact must be the negative of base-period traffic, and the newly opened-store impact must be positive current-period traffic; neither may be written as zero change.
- **25% `R5-餐段增量贡献链`**: Each of the six "period-daypart" chains is likewise scored by taking the `min` of two leaves, "absolute change" and "brand-wide contribution," and then averaging across chains. Each chain must reconcile from the three components of continuing-store change, newly opened-store inflow, and closed-store outflow for that daypart. For `2023→2024`: breakfast `-11/-22.9167%`, lunch `+21/43.7500%`, and dinner `+38/79.1667%`. For `2024→2025`: breakfast `+1/1.6949%`, lunch `+17/28.8136%`, and dinner `+41/69.4915%`. A daypart's own year-over-year rate or a change calculated only for continuing stores must not be presented as the brand-wide contribution.
- **10% `R6-桥接闭合与范围控制`**: Each comparison period is scored using five binary leaves `[0,1]`: the sum of the three portfolio impacts equals the brand-wide net change; the portfolio contributions sum to 100%; the sum of the three daypart changes equals the brand-wide net change; the daypart contributions sum to 100%; and each daypart reconciles across the continuing/newly opened/closed components. It must also be made explicit that continuing-store analysis is not the brand-wide total and that the latest operating status must not overwrite the preceding comparison period. Merely writing "reconciled" without showing the values on both sides receives no credit.
- **9% `R7-先结论后依据`**: Each comparison period is scored using five binary leaves `[0,1]`: an upfront total-volume conclusion and relative change, continuing-store change, newly opened-store uplift, closed-store offset, and itemized evidence for all three dayparts. The brand-wide totals are respectively `374→422 thousand visits, +48/+12.8342%` and `422→481 thousand visits, +59/+13.9810%`. Dinner is the largest daypart driver in both periods; breakfast is a net offset in the first period and a modest driver in the second. Merely stating that "store openings drove traffic" without the numerical denominator and the closed-store offset receives no credit.

## Equivalences and Boundaries

- Rounding of decimals and percentages within reasonable display precision is equivalent. Converting thousands of visits to visits receives credit as long as the entire chain is converted consistently and the unit is stated explicitly. Differences in sheet names, column names, sheet order, layout, formulas versus static values, and synonymous wording are not errors.
- Small differences explainable by display precision or unit conversion are not errors, but the relationship must be explained; `0.5` is not automatically interpreted as `0.5%`.
- When evidence is insufficient, retaining a formal unresolved state, listing conditional outcomes, and specifying concrete actions to obtain the missing evidence can receive full credit. Adopting a single result without evidence is a failure. Merely writing "to be confirmed" is not complete. The status information in these materials is complete, so an unresolved state must not be fabricated.
- Status boundaries apply only to the specified adjacent periods. A store closing in a later period does not erase its continuing-store record in an earlier comparison period. A store name continuing to appear also does not mean the store operated in both periods.
- The source table's year-over-year figures, like-for-like growth rates, and daypart mix percentages cannot replace absolute-change contributions. Merely copying all original columns can earn at most `R1-可用工作簿`, `R2-三期原值`, and the small amount of status credit actually supported.

## Deductions

- `D-素材回声` coefficient 0.45: The deliverable is substantively only a transcription of the original ledger, with most portfolio bridges, common denominators, signed contributions, daypart reconciliations, and conclusions absent. This does not apply when the main attribution chains are complete and only a few items are missing.
- `D-形式替代实质` coefficient 0.35: The deliverable consists mainly of empty tables, styling, screenshots, or placeholders, with material-specific values and relationships missing on a large scale. This does not apply when the data is complete but the formatting is plain.
- `D-候选倾倒` coefficient 0.40: The deliverable lists multiple mutually exclusive status assignments or conflicting denominator-based answers without identifying the adopted status and period. Conditional branches supported by the materials do not trigger this deduction.
- `D-自证替代证据` coefficient 0.30: The deliverable merely asserts "like-for-like comparability" or "portfolio reconciliation" without store-daypart details, values on both sides, changes, denominators, and contributions that allow recalculation. A small number of status labels alone does not trigger this deduction.
- `D-无关充数` coefficient 0.25: Content unrelated to traffic, store status, dayparts, and cross-period attribution occupies most of the deliverable, while the attribution for the two comparison periods is clearly incomplete. Necessary methodology notes and controls do not trigger this deduction.
